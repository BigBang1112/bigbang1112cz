ENVIMIX b4 2018 is a very special album release that collects some of my production before July 2018 (the release of the Nadeo Envimix title pack) and reworks them with my current production skills. All of the project files in this album have started between November 2016 and July 2018. The album was released as the official soundtrack for the ENVIMIX Turbo project in 2025. The style is very melodical and rhytmical just as I produced in the past and combines them with mixing techniques I've adapted many years later.
