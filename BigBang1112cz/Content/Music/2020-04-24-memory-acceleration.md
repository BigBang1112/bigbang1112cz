Memory Acceleration is my first completed album with 17 tracks. It focuses more on the melancholic side, but with major chords, forming a unique blend of experiences I was going through at that time. The album was in the works from 2018 to 2020 and features 2 lyrical tracks for the first time in my musical journey, sung by Rasmus Henneken.
