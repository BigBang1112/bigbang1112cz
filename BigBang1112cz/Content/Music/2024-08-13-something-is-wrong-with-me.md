This was my 3rd unsuccessful submission for All Nighter. It started with the piano chords and the beginning, and I had the idea to make an unexpected drop. The production flew really well and the song was completed in just 6 hours, though I could've made the third buildup a little better for sure. This song is about self-reflection, but struggling to figure it out, then feeling lost and not knowing what to do with yourself, perceiving unfairness strongly.
