Mutants is a menu music for the TrackMania Sunrise game. I have a lot of nostalgia connected with the song and the game, so this was the first bootleg I finished. It was the first time I attempted to share my music with a wider audience (my small Trackmania community around YouTube), and it was very uncomfortable to do, but I pushed through. The bootleg was perceived mostly alright, though the production wasn't really there yet. The original producer Dom Lyne commented on it as well, which was a huge encouragement for me at that time.
