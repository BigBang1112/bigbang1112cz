Delay Dawn is my first contest submission to the All Nighter competition. The goal was to make a song in 24 hours. I had an idea of using the delay effect creatively, resulting in such a track in 14 hours. Even though the song was made this quick, it was quite a struggle to get it right, though I wanted to sleep properly so I just finished it that night and submitted it. It didn't make it to the top 45, but the song was lucky to be placed as 17th track of their other submission playlist.
