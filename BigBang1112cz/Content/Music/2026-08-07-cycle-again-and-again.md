This single is a start of the next series of music I curated since the beginning of 2020, which peaks as an album called CAKEWALK coming in Summer 2027. Cycle has been the track I decided a long ago to be the starter of this new journey, due to its strong original sound that reminds of a comeback to life and its joys related. Again And Again is a more repetitive complement to Cycle with a bit more repetitive and melodic take.
